Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Short version: PlayMic runs on your PC and keeps your stuff there. Your audio, your recordings and your files never leave your computer. Here's the little that does, and why.

What PlayMic sends

  • Your license key and a PC code. When you activate PlayMic, and about once a day after that, it sends your key and a code made from your Windows installation ID. The code is a one-way scramble, so the ID itself never leaves your PC. We keep the key, the codes of the (up to 2) PCs that use it, and the date it was first activated. That's how a key works on your PCs and not on a hundred others.
  • Update checks. PlayMic asks our server if there's a new version. That request carries nothing about you, but like any web request it comes from your IP address.
  • Crash reports, only if you say yes. If PlayMic closes by itself, it asks you next time before sending anything. A report has the PlayMic and Windows version numbers, how long it was running, the error code and where in PlayMic's code it happened. No files, no names, no audio.
  • Problem reports, only when you send one. "Report a problem" (in Settings) sends what you type, the email or Discord name you add if you want an answer, the PlayMic and Windows version numbers, a short line about PlayMic's state (live or not, mic set up or not), the last 5 characters of your key, and the end of PlayMic's own logs. The logs list audio device IDs and what PlayMic did, never your sound or file names.

The free demo doesn't send anything at all.

What PlayMic never sends

Your microphone, the sound you share or play, your recordings and clips, your settings, your file names, and your Windows ID. PlayMic has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who handles it

  • Cloudflare runs our server and stores the license records. Like every web host, it sees your IP address when PlayMic connects. We keep an IP for up to 2 hours, and only to stop report spam.
  • Telegram carries crash reports and problem reports (only the ones you chose to send) to the developer.
  • itch.io handles your purchase, payment and email. We don't get your card details. See itch.io's privacy policy.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Why we're allowed to

License checks and updates are part of the product you bought, so we need them to give it to you. Crash reports and problem reports go out only when you choose to send them.

How long we keep it

License records stay while your key exists. Crash reports and problem reports are kept only as long as they help fix the problem, and we use your contact only to answer you.

Your choices and rights

  • Free up a PC any time: Settings, then License, then "Deactivate this PC". That removes its code from our records.
  • Turn off automatic updates in Settings.
  • Ask what we have about your key, or ask us to delete it, through email at girgis.it@gmail.com or the comments on PlayMic's itch.io page. Mention your key (just the last 5 characters, never post the whole key in a comment) and we'll sort it out.
  • If you're in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Kids

PlayMic isn't made for children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also show up in PlayMic's "What's new".
